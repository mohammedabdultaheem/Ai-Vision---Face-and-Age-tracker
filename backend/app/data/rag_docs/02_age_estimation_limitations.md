# Age Estimation Limitations & Ethical AI Boundaries

## Fundamental Limitations
1. **Biological vs. Morphological Discrepancy**:
   - Machine learning age estimation models evaluate apparent visual age based on facial surface morphology (e.g., skin elasticity, wrinkles, facial bone structure, lighting).
   - Visual age varies significantly due to genetic factors, health, sleep, skincare, cosmetic products, facial hair, and expression.
   - Predictions must always be treated as rough statistical approximations, never as legal proof of biological age or chronological verification.

2. **Quality Sensitivity**:
   - Severe lighting conditions (<45 or >220 brightness) distort facial textures, skewing age predictions.
   - Motion blur or low resolution (<50x50px crop) removes critical fine-grained facial features, typically biasing predictions towards median adult cohorts.

3. **Strict Ethical AI Boundaries**:
   - AI VISION strictly prohibits inferring sensitive demographic attributes, including race, ethnicity, religion, gender identity, genetic traits, or medical conditions.
   - The platform never performs individual identity recognition, matching, or persistent biometric identification.
